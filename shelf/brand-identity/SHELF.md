# brand-identity (shelved 2026-11-22)

**Source.** github.com/fatihaydost/brand-identity-skill at `af7ba40`, `skills/brand-identity/`, MIT (`LICENSE`). `TRADEMARKS.md` notes its colour library holds third-party brand data.

**Copied.** `SKILL.md` and `references/`, unchanged. **Left out** (fetch from the pinned commit when needed): `scripts/` (1.2 MB of Python), `templates/`, `assets/`, `evals/`.

**What it does.** Over a 16 to 20 turn run it builds a new brand: a discovery brief, logo directions, type and palette sets checked by measured gates, and a PDF guidelines kit. It needs `uv` with fonttools, uharfbuzz and skia-pathops, downloads from Google Fonts and GitHub, and a headless browser that opens your site and any competitor sites you name.

**Why not now.** Geniotek's brand is settled, and the designer already has `frontend-design`'s list of generated-look defaults.

**Kept for.** OS Flow's vision that each business user builds their own brand identity (Jase, 2026-11-22). Worth one idea sooner: its glyph-coverage gate per brand language, since Archivo and IBM Plex Sans have no Chinese glyphs.
